R03 consequence:
The SDK's blocking spin_lock wrapper disables preemption before arch_spin_lock.
R03 therefore does not embed the blocking loop. It uses one atomic testset attempt
and branches directly to its return path on failure, so interrupt or same-core
reentry cannot spin while the current producer is preempted.
