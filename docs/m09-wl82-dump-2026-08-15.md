## Why the Hashes Don't Match — RESOLVED

The forced-loader dump returns **physical flash bytes** (ciphertext), the same
representation as the live USB dumps. The FWSC hashes were computed from the
unpacked `.fwsc` file's `flash.bin` view, which stores the app area in
`sfc_cipher` form with base `0x4000` and key `0x980F`.

The six recovery-candidate sectors span regions with different cipher
treatment:

| Address | Region | Why Hash Differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0x04000` | JLFS header | Package stores plain; live stores `plain ^ lfsr(0x132b)` |
| `0x20000`–`0x5a000` | App area | Both are `sfc_cipher` but M09 ran modified firmware |
| `0x99000` | App area tail | Same: M09 ran modified firmware, different bytes |

The hash mismatch is **expected** and does not indicate a dump failure. The
dump representation is now validated as physical ciphertext. See
`docs/flash-layout-cipher-analysis.md` for the full region map and cipher
details.
